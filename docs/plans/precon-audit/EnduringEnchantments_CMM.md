## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Anikthea, a WBG enchantment creature with menace, exiles an enchantment from your graveyard on entering or attacking and makes a 3/3 Zombie copy. The deck fills the yard (self-mill/discard), reanimates enchantments and uses constellation/enchantress payoffs. Sources differ on whether to keep self-mill (1 cuts it for discard, 2 adds more).
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Anikthea only reanimates one enchantment at a time, so self-mill adds little (1)
- Needs heavy discard/self-mill to be reliable (2)
- Can't return Auras; some ETBs are lost on reanimation (2)
- Tokens are 3/3 creatures and exposed to removal (2)
- Cheap enchantments are scarce, so enchantress creatures are slow (2)
- Precon mana base is only passable; many upgrades are expensive (3)

## Consensus one-liner
Swap the generic land ramp and off-theme filler for enchantment ramp, protection and strong enchantment payoffs that Anikthea can reanimate.
