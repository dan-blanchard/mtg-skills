## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Felothar lets high-toughness creatures and defenders attack, assign combat damage by toughness, and draw cards. Both articles remove the lifegain subtheme (it mostly served the alternate commander Betor) and lean into walls, toughness, protection and one-sided sweepers.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Lifegain subtheme takes space and mainly supports Betor (1, 2)
- Mana base: tapped lands and too many basics, "the usual sins" (1)
- Light, suboptimal interaction (1, 2)
- Under-supported themes and weak individual cards (1)
- Expensive and low-impact low drops (2)
- Seedborn Muse is kill-on-sight and has little to spend mana on (1, 2)
- Weak reach; Tip the Scales is soft early (2)

## Consensus one-liner
Drop the lifegain/Betor package and Seedborn Muse for cheaper toughness bodies, protection, and one-sided sweepers like Dusk // Dawn.
