## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Rakdos group slug: ping, drain and punish opponents so they lose life on their own turns. Valgavoth gets a counter and draws when that happens (up to three times per turn cycle). Articles add more life-loss/punisher effects, lifegain or lifegain denial, protection for the commander, and a better mana base.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Lackluster new cards from the set (1)
- No graveyard recursion or hand-size enhancers despite heavy draw (1)
- Valgavoth will be removed often and needs protection (2)
- Card draw and removal weaker than needed; no staples such as Demonic Tutor (2)
- Weak mana base: tapped lands, Evolving Wilds, Terramorphic Expanse (1, 3)
- Cards that ping without efficiently triggering Valgavoth (2)
- Sower of Discord weak in two-player games (1)

## Consensus one-liner
Swap the weak set cards and tapped lands for efficient life-loss punishers that trigger Valgavoth every opponent turn (Roiling Vortex, Rug of Smothering, Razorkin Needlehead, Zo-Zu) plus protection and a faster mana base.
