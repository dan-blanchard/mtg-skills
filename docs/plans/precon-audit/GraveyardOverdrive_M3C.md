## Sources
1. Graveyard Overdrive Upgrade Guide - Modern Horizons 3 — EDHREC (Andy Zupke) — https://cloudflare.edhrec.com/articles/graveyard-overdrive-upgrade-guide-modern-horizons-3 — 2024-06-13 — budget tier cards $5 or less each; non-budget tier above that
2. Graveyard Overdrive Precon Upgrade Guide — Card Kingdom Blog (Kristen Gregory) — https://blog.cardkingdom.com/graveyard-overdrive-modern-horizons-3-commander-precon-upgrade-guide/ — 2024-06-07 — about $50 package, plus further upgrades
(Cut-Rate Commander also has a video guide, https://edhrec.com/articles/?p=135070, not readable as text.)
## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
A Jund Lhurgoyf deck: Disa the Restless puts milled or discarded Lhurgoyfs onto the battlefield (and has a combat-damage trigger). Upgrades add more Lhurgoyfs/changelings, self-mill, card draw, token value, recursion, and a spread of card types for Lhurgoyf size.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Lacks focus and card draw (1)
- A little light on removal (2)
- Depends on Disa surviving (2)
- Vulnerable to graveyard exile and commander removal (2)
- Needs more ways to fill the graveyard (1, 2)

## Consensus one-liner
Both articles say to add more self-mill, recursion and card draw (Ripples of Undeath, Buried Alive, Gix) to feed Disa's graveyard trigger.
