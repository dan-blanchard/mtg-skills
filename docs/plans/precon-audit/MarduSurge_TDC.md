## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Zurgo Stormrender drives a Warriors/token deck built on mobilize and myriad: draw when tokens leave as attackers, drain otherwise. Draftsim makes it leaner and more aggressive with anthems, Impact Tremors effects and protection; Card Kingdom adds token makers and sweeper-style payoffs. Neriv, Crackling Vanguard is the alternate commander.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Conflicting themes: many sacrifice outlets clash with token cards (1)
- Identity crisis: midrange top-end (Myr Battlesphere, Legion Loyalty) vs aggro plan (1)
- Easily swept; needs protection (1)
- Weak reach and interaction (1)
- Expensive cards that are hard to cast consistently (Legion Loyalty, Siege-Gang Commander) (2)
- Zurgo's draw requires attacking, so early game is awkward (2)
- Precon mana base (1)
