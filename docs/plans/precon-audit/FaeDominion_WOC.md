## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Dimir Faeries: Tegwyll is a Faerie lord for go-wide sacrifice/card-draw value; the alternate commander Alela wants flash and flying tempo with instant-speed spells. Articles say the precon is effectively two decks and should commit to one (Tegwyll wide, or Alela flash).
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Two decks in one; must pick a direction (3)
- Too few flash Faeries for an Alela plan (3)
- Weak instant suite (3)
- Too many basics (about 25; 39 lands is too many) (3)
- Lacks acceleration and explosiveness; few ways to push damage and win (1)
- Little protection beyond counterspells (1)
- High-MV cards (Oona, Hullbreaker Horror) hard to support (1, 3)

## Consensus one-liner
Pick one direction and commit, adding Faerie staples (Bitterblossom, Spellstutter Sprite, Mistbind Clique, Notorious Throng) while cutting clunky high-cost cards like Hullbreaker Horror and trimming basics.
