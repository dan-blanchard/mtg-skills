## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Izzet spells. Stella Lee exiles the top card when you cast a second spell each turn; with three or more spells she can copy an instant or sorcery, and with untap effects that loops into a win. Card Kingdom (1) pushes a Stella combo build with untappers and tutors. Draftsim (2) instead builds a spells-into-tokens deck with Guttersnipe-style damage.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Stella's combo needs two other spells in a turn plus spare mana, which is hard to set up (1).
- Lacks fast mana typical of spellslingers (1).
- Needs more untap wincons for consistent access (1).
- Eris, the backup commander, is lower power than Stella (1).
- Focus is split across storm, burn, prowess and tokens (2).
- Too few lands: 27 basics, weak mana base (2).
- Light on removal; board wipes threaten the token plan (2).

## Consensus one-liner
The two usable articles disagree on plan (untap combo vs. token spells), but both want cheaper cantrips and mana, better lands and more instant-speed interaction in place of clunky permanents.
