2. Animated Army Upgrade Guide — Draftsim (A.L. Walser) — https://draftsim.com/animated-army-upgrade-guide/ — 2024-07-24, updated 2024-09-07 — no upgrade budget stated
(Cut-Rate Commander video, https://edhrec.com/articles/?p=137628, not readable as text: fix Bello being removed and too few creatures; lean on enchantress payoffs.)
## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Gruul: on your turn Bello turns non-Aura enchantments and non-Equipment artifacts with mana value 4+ into 4/4 haste, indestructible Elementals. The deck ramps into big artifacts/enchantments and attacks. Both articles drop the token subtheme and add protection, card draw, anthems and interaction.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Token subtheme covers only a few cards and slows the deck (1, 2)
- Needs protection for creatures and Bello (1)
- Minimal interaction beyond board wipes (2)
- Off-theme cards kept to give them a home (2)
- Few blockers on opponents' turns since Elementals stop being creatures (2, reader comment)

## Consensus one-liner
Cut the token subtheme and filler, then add protection (Asceticism, Boots, Heroic Intervention), card draw and cards that make more Bello animations (Guardian Project, Chimil).
