## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
The deck adds and removes time counters, with Suspend and Vanishing as the engine; the Tenth Doctor supplies counters and Rose Tyler grows. Both articles strip the token/Clue/Soldier subtheme and focus on time travel. Endgames named: extra turns and a big Rose attack (2), The Millennium Calendar as an alternate win (1).
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Too few cards manipulate time counters besides the commander (2)
- The Tenth Doctor needs protection from removal (2)
- Token cards (Soldiers, Clues) don't synergize with the time plan; reads like two precons merged (2)
- Cards that neither add nor remove time counters are dead weight (1)
- Weak mana base: underwhelming lands in Jeskai colors (2)
- Hard to keep up with everything on the board; the deck is a headache to pilot (1)

## Consensus one-liner
Cut everything that doesn't add or remove time counters (tokens, companions, Sagas) and replace it with Suspend/Vanishing and counter-manipulation cards.
