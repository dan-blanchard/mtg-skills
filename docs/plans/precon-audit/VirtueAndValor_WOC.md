## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Selesnya Enchantress/auras: Ellivere makes Virtuous Roles, draws cards and pumps the team by enchantment count, with go-wide anthems. Secondary themes: Role tokens, blinking Ellivere, trample/lifelink/evasion auras.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Mana base: ~30 basics and only 5 duals; double-white/green unreliable (2)
- Weak ramp (Paradise Druid, Sylvan Ranger, Utopia Sprawl, Fertile Ground) (2)
- More creatures than auras; clunky cards (Celestial Archon, Realm-Cloaked Giant) (2)
- Only seven non-permanent spells, too few interaction/wipes (3)
- Ellivere can't target herself, so she may stay out of combat (3)
- Introduces Role tokens without using all Roles (3)
- A few questionable card choices (1)

## Consensus one-liner
Fix the mana base and ramp, swap clunky creatures and weak enchantments for enchantress card draw (Sythis), protection and on-theme auras.
