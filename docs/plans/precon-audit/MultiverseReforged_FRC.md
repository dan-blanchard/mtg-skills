## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
A four-color (no green) polymorph deck: cheap noncreature token makers feed Jace's polymorph, Mass Polymorph and similar effects, which turn tokens into big creatures or planeswalkers. All articles keep Jace over Nissa, Leyline Tamer. Secondary themes are planeswalker value, blink loops (Aminatou/Felidar Guardian) and stealing.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Jace polymorphs once, then sits at low loyalty and likely dies (1, 3)
- Thin creature count (18) starting at six mana; few token payoffs (4)
- Too few cheap token makers and bodies for polymorph (3)
- Heavy dependence on the commander; only three cards do the core effect (4)
- Bombs designed for other decks that do not fit: Omnath, Akroma, Tamiyo, Ginger (1, 2, 4)
- Mana base weak: only two fetch lands, Path of Ancestry does nothing (1)
- Top-heavy: several six-plus mana cards (2)
- Polymorph can whiff into dead cards like Venser (2, 4)

## Consensus one-liner
Cut the off-plan bombs (Omnath, Akroma, Niv-Mizzet, Dack) for cheap token makers, repeatable polymorph (Identity Echo) and Jace protection or blink support.
