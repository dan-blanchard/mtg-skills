## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
A Naya landfall and lands-matter deck. Yuma gets cheaper per land in the graveyard and lets you sacrifice a land to draw; Deserts in the graveyard make 4/2 Plants. Both guides want a more classic lands deck built on graveyard land recursion (Crucible, Loam) with token and damage payoffs.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Payoffs are creature-centric and fragile to removal (3).
- No built-in protection for Yuma (search snippet of 1).
- Vulnerable to land destruction like Armageddon (2).
- Weak mana base: too many basics, no fixing or real payoff land (3).
- Several recursion cards are costly with mediocre bodies (2).

## Consensus one-liner
The deck's biggest gain is land recursion (Life from the Loam, Crucible of Worlds) with better lands and protection against its fragility.
