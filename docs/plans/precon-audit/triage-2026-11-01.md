# Triage of the never-admitted guide adds (2026-11-01)

Source: `~/mtg/tuner-work/w41/reach-profile-N11.md`, "The 84 never admitted, by deck"
(held-out 20, baseline N11). Excluded from this pass: Ahoy Mateys' 13 (the tribe-profile
read) and the 10 other tutors (Demonic ×3, Worldly ×2, Vampiric, Enlightened Tutor,
Gamble, Natural Order, Cateran Summons: the tutor need read). That leaves **61 cards**
in 19 decks (the backlog's "53" was an estimate). Two read gaps found while packaging the
upstream reports are in the pass too (the last two rows).

Method: for each card, the deck's commander text, its `commander_needs`, the tune's pages
(`w41/misses-ho-N11deep.json`), the card's signals, and which page would hold it. A
**recall gap** is a read the deck's plan should admit; it is fixed as a read, with a
real-card test. A **guide-specific** pick is one no read of the deck's cards or the
commander's text can justify: the first-principles ceiling. "Paged" below means the
need it now meets is at most `BROAD_SHARE` (10%) of the identity's nonland pool, so
`need_avenues` opens a page for it (shares measured over the commander-legal identity
pool, not by a tune).

Every rules claim below was checked with `rules-lookup` / `rulings-lookup` (CR
2026-09-25).

## Summary

- 61 cards triaged: **21 recall gaps fixed** (Chocobo Knights, The Ozolith ×3, Sword
  Coast Sailor, Flaming Fist, Duelist's Heritage, Junk Winder, Metastatic Evangel,
  Patrolling Peacemaker, Heliod, Mirrormind Crown, Slumbering Keepguard, Grim Guardian,
  Victor, Boon of the Spirit Realm, Ondu Spiritdancer, Maelstrom Wanderer, Life from the
  Loam, Kalakscion), **1 recall gap not fixed** (Secret Arcade // Dusty Parlor), **39
  guide-specific** (the first-principles ceiling).
- Both read gaps (a) and (b) fixed.
- Pages the fixes open or change (measured over the identity pool, not by a tune): new
  narrow pages for "token copy consumers" (Satya 58, Ashling 134), "loyalty on the
  commander" (Quintorius 21), "entering enchantment payoffs like the commander" (Killian
  32), "creature spells with power 6+" (Eshki 780; the 4+ need is broad), "-1/-1 counters
  put on creatures" (Auntie 735, was the broad "counter_added makers"), "evasion and double
  strike for the commander" (Sidar 381, was the broad "evasion for your attackers"); a
  narrower Ashling "creatures that sacrificed" (no token makers: 1,900, 5.8%, now paged,
  commit 438c0b1f); a wider "plus one consumers" (Captain America: the N11 tune's page
  held 172, the identity pool now 207), "energy makers" (Satya: 101 then, 149 now), "land
  sacrifice consumers" (Hearthhull: 11 then, 83 now). Hearthhull loses its mislabelled
  "creatures that sacrificed". None was measured on the gate; the reads ship on
  correctness, as the plan says. Admission was checked against the need pages
  (`satisfies` over the identity pool), not by a tune, so the parent's scoreboard run is
  the first measure of what the pages' order does with them.

## Phase misparses found (for upstream)

- **Kaust, Eyes of the Glade**: "Whenever a creature you control that was turned face up
  this turn deals combat damage to a player, draw a card" is typed as
  `mode: {Unknown: "Whenever a creature you control that was turned face up this turn
  deals combat damage to a player"}` with no `valid_source` / `valid_target`. Expected a
  `DamageDone` (combat, to a player) trigger whose source is a creature you control turned
  face up this turn.
- **Mirrormind Crown**: "As long as this Equipment is attached to a creature, the first
  time you would create one or more tokens each turn, you may instead create that many
  tokens that are copies of equipped creature" is a Spell ability whose effect is
  `Unimplemented{name: as_long_as_gate}`. Expected a conditional `CreateToken` replacement
  (as Anointed Procession's, `token_owner_scope: You`) whose tokens are copies of the
  equipped creature. Served meanwhile by the ledger row `token_creation_replacement_parked`.
- Not a misparse, noted: in a `SpellCast` trigger phase names the cast spell
  `CostPaidObject` (Eshki's "that spell's power", Cabaretti Revels', Jodah's "lesser mana
  value"); the power read keys on it.
- Kalakscion, Hunger Tyrant has no oracle text in MTGJSON or phase's card-data because it
  has none: its only DFT printing (uncommon) carries flavor text alone, and
  `rulings-lookup` finds no rulings. A vanilla 7/2, not a data gap.

## Follow-ups (2026-11-01, second pass)

- The same `OwnersOfCardsExiledBySource` actor fed three doer lanes that read the effect
  node alone: `seek_matters|you` on Darkstar Banisher and Cackling Observer (the owner
  seeks), `free_cast|you` on Spell Queller (the owner casts), `facedown_makers|you` on
  Unidentified Hovership (the owner manifests dread). Fixed in 5a937f56 through the shared
  `crosswalk.effects_by_actor`: each now reads "opponents". The seek serve pin moved to
  Cabaretti Revels, with Darkstar Banisher dropped.
- Fixed in a third pass (9af18cd4): Reality Shift's "Exile target creature. Its controller
  manifests the top card of their library" read `facedown_makers|you|` (its manifest
  targets `ParentTargetController`, and the record's `Manifest` keyword fired the
  keyword-array arm). The shared actor read now counts the controller or owner of the
  object the chain answers as the opponents (`crosswalk.acts_for_answered_target`, inside
  `acted_by_opponents` / `effects_by_actor`, as Beast Within's token reads), and the
  keyword array anchors a face-down keyword action only when phase types no node for it:
  Reality Shift reads `facedown_makers|opponents|`. Unidentified Hovership's owner's
  manifest dread fills the owner's graveyard (CR 701.62a), so `self_graveyard_fills`
  skips another player's manifest dread or surveil through the same read: no
  `graveyard_makers|you|`, and no `facedown_matters|you|` either.
