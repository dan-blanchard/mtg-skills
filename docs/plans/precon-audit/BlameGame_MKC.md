## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Boros goad deck: Nelly Borca rewards opponents (and you) for damage dealt by their creatures to other opponents, so goad and forced-combat cards keep the table hitting each other. Draftsim calls it midrange verging on control with deterrents and Fog-like effects. Upgrades add rewards for opponents' attacks/actions, card advantage, and a win condition beyond goad.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Mostly medium-synergy pieces; goad alone is hard to win with (1)
- Very little removal; relies on goad and Fog effects like Gideon's Sacrifice (2)
- Some deterrents weak or inefficient; lags behind other MKM precons (2)
- Nelly must attack each turn and can be blocked/taxed out by removal (1, 3)
- Feather, Radiant Arbiter has few creature-targeting spells to support it (1, 2, 3)
- Many mediocre topdecks, high mana costs (1)
- Mana base: basics and tapped utility lands cause tempo loss (2, 3)

## Consensus one-liner
Add more goad/deterrent payoffs and cheap interaction plus a real finisher (Aurelia, Agrus Kos, Taunt from the Rampart), and cut the weak or off-plan cards like Feather, Sun Titan and Ransom Note.
