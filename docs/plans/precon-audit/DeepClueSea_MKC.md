## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Bant Clue deck. Morska has no max hand size, investigates each upkeep and gets two +1/+1 counters when you draw your second card each turn. The deck makes Clues, cracks them for cards and mana, and wins through counters and Clue payoffs.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Slow mana base is the biggest drawback (1)
- Light on removal; few counterspells (3)
- High curve (3)
- Artifact removal and mill threaten the plan; counter growth depends on card draw (4)
- Several new cards are weak or "dead ends" (1)
- Risk of losing several value pieces at once (3)

## Consensus one-liner
Cut the off-plan creatures and dead-end spells, then add Clue doublers and mana sinks plus counterspells and better lands to fix the slow mana and light interaction.
