## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Naya Dinosaur ramp: get big Dinosaurs out and trigger Pantlaza's discover every turn (Pantlaza over backup commander Wayta, Trainer Prodigy). Goes tall rather than wide. Secondary themes: token makers to trigger discover, enrage (Wayta), recursion to stack the library top.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Slow, high average MV (4.08), easily demolished by board wipes (1)
- Ramp never felt sufficient given the high mana values (1)
- Needs more protection and cheap removal (1)
- Several less-than-ideal cards; Marauding Raptor kills some of the deck's own cards (3)
- Wayta's enrage synergy lacks critical mass (2)
- Dino DNA slow at making tokens (2)

## Consensus one-liner
Cut clunky, high-MV and low-impact cards for cheap ramp, protection and recursion plus stronger Dinosaur payoffs like Ghalta and Mavren and Gishath.
