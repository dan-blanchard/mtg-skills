## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Jeskai (URW) superfriends. Guff is himself a planeswalker, adds loyalty counters to another planeswalker each end step and has a draw/damage -3. The deck lands many planeswalkers early and protects them with fogs, walls and pillowfort pieces.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Needs more ways to close out games (1)
- Planeswalkers are mana-expensive (1)
- Planeswalkers are vulnerable to attacks in multiplayer (4)
- Underperforming originals: Jace, Mirror Mage; Deploy the Gatewatch; Norn's Annex (1)
- Few sacrifice outlets, no counterspells, only passable mana (3)

## Consensus one-liner
Replace the weak originals and conditional ramp with stronger planeswalkers, proliferate/loyalty payoffs (Elspeth's Talent, All Will Be One, Mila) and better ways to protect them and close out games.
