## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Five-color Sliver typal aggro. Sliver Gravemother removes the legend rule for Slivers and gives Slivers in the graveyard Encore X, so cheap Slivers get into the yard and come back as hasty tokens; team-wide Sliver effects stack on a wide board.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Five-color mana base is the main weakness; many lands enter tapped (1, 2, 3)
- Support and card draw are thin, so the deck is inconsistent (1, 2)
- Few ways to put Slivers in the graveyard besides dying (2)
- Going wide loses to board wipes (2, 3)
- No tutors (3)
- Some cards are dead or awkward depending on board (1)

## Consensus one-liner
Fix the five-color mana base and add draw, protection and cheap encore-friendly Slivers while cutting the clunky wipes and redundant Slivers.
