## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Saheeli gets an energy counter whenever you cast an artifact or Artificer spell, then pays three at combat to copy a permanent as a 5/5 haste artifact creature sacrificed at end step. The articles push the deck toward "big energy" and copy-worthy permanents, with token doublers, artifact recursion, and fewer Thopter-specific cards. Good copy targets and ways to keep the token (Sundial of the Infinite) are the named secondary themes.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Too few energy payoffs and generators; the precon "was a bit lacking in recursion" (3)
- Mana base is shaky for three colors, with weak lands (2, 1)
- Thopter/artificer filler that does not feed the copy plan (1, 2)
- Top-end it cannot ramp into (Triplicate Titan, Duplicant) (2)
- Creature-heavy and exposed; needs commander protection (2)
- Saheeli's copy is sacrificed at end of turn (3)
- Several cards are weak without Saheeli on board (2)

## Consensus one-liner
Add more energy generators and doublers (Generatorium, Aether Refinery, HELIOS One) and better copy targets while trimming Thopter filler and weak lands.
