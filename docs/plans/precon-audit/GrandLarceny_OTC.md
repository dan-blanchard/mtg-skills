## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Sultai theft. Gonti's trigger exiles and lets you play cards from an opponent's library when your creatures deal combat damage, so the deck wants evasive creatures plus mana to cast stolen cards. Theft doubles as card draw in a grindy midrange shell; the non-budget tier leans into Rogues.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Lacks small, evasive creatures to get Gonti triggers (1).
- Lacks cheap ramp despite a high curve (3).
- Many steal effects need extra mana to cast the stolen cards (2).
- Exiling can hit lands or unhelpful cards (2).
- Some creature choices are slow or off-plan, e.g. Oblivion Sower and Void Attendant (3).

## Consensus one-liner
Add cheap unblockable creatures and cheap ramp, cut the slow filler, and lean on Court of Locthwain and exile effects to keep Gonti's theft flowing.
