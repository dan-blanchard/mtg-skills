## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Colorless Eldrazi ramp deck. Zhulodok gives colorless spells of mana value 7+ cast from hand a double Cascade. The deck ramps with rocks and lands into huge Eldrazi and cascades into more.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Slow, top-heavy curve; hard to speed up on a budget (1, 2)
- Early turns rely on mana rocks; exposed to aggression (4)
- X spells and 12-mana Rise of the Eldrazi are poor Cascade hits (1, 4)
- Few interactive spells, little removal, few wipes (2)
- Colorless restriction limits tools (2)
- Exposed to artifact removal on Zhulodok (4)

## Consensus one-liner
Cut the X spells and weak filler, add cheap rocks, utility lands and card draw so the deck reaches its 7+ drops and cascades more reliably.
