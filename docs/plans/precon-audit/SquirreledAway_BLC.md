## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Golgari tokens: Hazel taps untapped tokens (paying life) for mana and copies a token at end step. The articles shift toward token creation and sacrifice (or token/Squirrel payoffs) rather than Food and a muddled aggro/sac split, using Hazel's mana for big spells and finishers.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Conflict between an aggressive token army and sacrificing tokens for value (1, 3)
- Lack of focus: token, sacrifice, Squirrel and Food themes all thin (3)
- Missing protection and recursion (1)
- Hazel makes lots of mana but few cards use it (3)
- Weak top end / few finishers (3)
- Off-theme Food cards such as Gourmand's Talent (2, 3)
- Hazel can only copy a token at end of your turn; Scurry of Squirrels tokens are exiled and can't be copied (2)

## Consensus one-liner
Pick one lane (tokens, with Squirrels and Food de-emphasized), cut the off-theme Food and filler cards, and add token makers, doublers and finishers (Parallel Lives, Craterhoof, Scute Swarm, Bitterblossom).
