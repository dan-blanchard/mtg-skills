## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Orzhov Vampires aristocrats: Clavileño makes Vampires into Demons by sacrificing smaller Vampires, so the deck needs sacrifice outlets and fodder. Secondary themes: reanimation, tokens, and combo (Vito + Exquisite Blood / Sanguine Bond).
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Needs many more sacrifice outlets to keep Clavileño going (1)
- Needs better Vampires and stronger sacrifice cards (2)
- Removal is tired (1)
- High-MV, durdly cards (1)
- Mana base needs work (2)
- Carmen, Cruel Skymarcher is 5 mana and easily removed (1)
- No real Demon support except Master of Dark Rites (2)

## Consensus one-liner
Add more sacrifice outlets, reanimation, better removal and Vampire payoffs (Vito, Sanguine Bond) while trimming clunky high-MV cards and tapped lands.
