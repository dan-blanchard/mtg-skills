## Deck plan (as the articles describe it)
Golgari self-mill and reanimation. Winter's attack mill and delirium ability (four or more card types in your graveyard) exile or return the best permanents, so the deck wants two-type permanents, graveyard fillers and strong reanimation targets. Card Kingdom also pitches assembling infinite combos once defenses are down. Rendmaw, Creaking Nest is the backup commander.
## Weaknesses the articles name
- Mill and delirium payoffs are weak, closer to Limited-level cards (1).
- Too many one-off self-mill effects that can mill key spells; the deck needs more focus (3).
- Not enough reanimation targets (3).
- Delirium is slow, does not scale and is not a win condition by itself; the plan is grindy and variance-heavy (2).
- Winter relies heavily on its own ability (1).
- Archenemy schemes are unconnected to the strategy (1).
- Creatures need to be cut more than other types to balance delirium (2).

## Consensus one-liner
Swap the weak one-type creatures and one-shot mill spells for two-type permanents and repeatable mill or reanimation that keeps delirium on.
