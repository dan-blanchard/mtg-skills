## How the gates were applied

**How phase marks a card unsupported.** Phase computes support per face in `crates/engine/src/game/coverage.rs` (`compute_card_face_coverage`). A face is supported only when it has no gap. The same function drives in-app deck validation (`card_face_gaps`) and the published report. Gaps are any of:

- an `Unimplemented` effect or cost;
- a trigger mode with no matcher (every `Unknown` mode);
- a static mode with no handler (`Unrecognized` conditions, `Other` modes);
- an unknown keyword;
- a `SwallowedClause` parse warning (`Swallow:*`);
- an invalid `AddSubtype` (`ParserMisfire:InvalidSubtype`);
- a resolver feature the engine doesn't handle;
- a silent-drop line count;
- a `metadata.confirmed_misparse` stamp.

The stamp comes from `crates/phase-ast/data/confirmed-misparses.toml`, a census of about 2,700 faces with the broken clause quoted. Phase holds every listed face unsupported until its fix lands.

**Rule 2 (parked clauses).** A parked clause always makes its card unsupported, so phase never treats such a card as fully playable. A parked draft was therefore filed only as a regression: its clause had to be typed, correctly or at least playably, at an earlier tag. Every parked card was checked at seven tags. None qualified. Necromancy was typed through v0.94.0 and has been parked since v0.104.0, but phase's own test pins that demotion as a deliberate fail-closed choice, and #7619 (open) reports the typed version misbehaving.

Several other parked cards were typed at older tags, but their typing was wrong (a dropped qualifier, half a compound event, a wrong count). Phase changed them to fail closed on purpose, so they are not regressions. These were: Hero of Bretagard and Ranar, Geistlight Snare, Blackmail, Even the Odds, Turtles Forever, Wall of Shadows, Disorder, Incite Rebellion, Aether Refinery, the "this way" and "for each" cards of drafts 31 to 36, Veiling Oddity, Tenuous Truce and Soul Partition.

The rule's second arm (a card phase treats as fully playable with a clause silently missing) would have caught the aftermath cards, which phase reports supported with their second half missing. An open issue already covers them.

**Rule 1 (silent misparses).** A typed clause that phase's census already quotes was not filed: it duplicates phase's own record. A silent misparse on a card phase holds unsupported for an unrelated gap was filed: Variable Solutions, Grim Reminder and Blood Lust. Can't Stay Away was dropped, because the coverage node holding its whole first line is itself marked unsupported. One exception: Eye of Singularity's enters trigger, where the card is unsupported anyway and `docs/parser-misparse-backlog.md` lists it under root cause 1.

**Engine behaviour.** Where a draft claimed a wrong game outcome, the claim was checked against phase's resolver code (`effects/mod.rs`, `targeting.rs`, `token_presets.rs`) at v0.133.0. Four drafts are wrong about the engine; they are marked "ours".

## Found beyond the list (not staged)

**"Each of those creatures deals damage equal to its power to [~]"** is typed as a `DealDamage` with no `damage_source`, so the ability's own source deals that damage. This affects Polukranos, World Eater, Living Inferno, Ravenous Gigantotherium and Alpha Brawl, all `supported: true` in the coverage export, and none in the census.

- **Parse:** Polukranos's trigger is a distributed `DealDamage X` to target creatures, then `DealDamage {amount: Power{Anaphoric}, target: SelfRef}` with no `damage_source`. Living Inferno and Ravenous Gigantotherium have the same chain.
- **Engine:** with no `damage_source`, `single_damage_source` uses `damage_context_from_ability`, so the dealer is the ability's source. Separately, `damaged_object_context_from_events` (`effects/mod.rs`) declines to name a "that creature" after a multi-target damage parent; its comment says "Living Inferno is intentionally NOT fixed here".
- **Ruling (Polukranos, 2013-09-15):** "As Polukranos's triggered ability resolves, Polukranos deals damage first, then the target creatures do."
- **Tracker:** #9749 (open), "Alpha Brawl: second clause ('each of those creatures deals damage equal to its power to that creature') is not applied", reports this clause on Alpha Brawl. Its "likely seam" (a tracked-set anaphor whose per-member power damage goes back to the clause-1 source) is the same root cause. #8684 (open) names the other three cards but is a different bug (a fizzle check).
- **Suggestion:** a short comment on #9749 naming Polukranos, Living Inferno and Ravenous Gigantotherium as further supported cards with the same clause could be proposed to the owner (not posted).
