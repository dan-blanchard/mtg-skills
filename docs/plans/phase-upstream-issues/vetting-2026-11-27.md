Copied from the batch-5 working notes (`~/.cache/mtg-skills/phase-bump/v0.133.0/upstream/batch-5/`, where `screen.json`, `probe/` and the staged `issues/01` live); the backlog reads block of 2026-11-27 acted on its "Workarounds to retire or rewrite" list.


**Newest release: v0.134.0**, unchanged since batch 3. Every check uses its card data, coverage export (`card_data_hash` `0fd43dd7cf32923f`), census (`confirmed-misparses.toml` at the tag) and engine. The files are the batch-3 copies.

## Method

- **Inputs:** 146 ledger rows (`bridge_ledger.BRIDGES`, the ids in `ledger-never-vetted-prescreen.tsv`) and 59 canaries (`canaries-never-vetted-prescreen.tsv`).
  - Each canary's cards come from its own test: literals, plus the module constants it is parametrized over (`canaries.json`).
- **Per card** (`screen.py` → `screen.json`): coverage `supported` and every gap; the census row by EXACT face key, which fixes the pre-screen's prefix over-match; and whether the parse changed from v0.133.0.
  - Only one pin's parse changed: Dalkovan Outrider, still unsupported with its gap.
- **Matching the clause:** `autoverdict.py` matched each workaround's own text (the row's `todo`/`census`, the canary's docstring) against the gap's and the census row's quoted clause.
  - 41 rows matched weakly. I re-read each one by hand (`auto-review.txt`), and every gap or census row is on the workaround's clause, or is a silent-drop count covering it.
- **By hand:** every row with a supported, non-census pin, read against the record, the engine source, and phase's tests or a measurement (`probe/probe_batch5.rs`, output in `probe/probe-output-v0.134.0.txt`).
- **Regressions:**
  - The hand-checked cards (Duneblast, Mount Doom, Anhelo, Silverquill, Solid Footing, Ram Through, Bumi's Feast Lecture, Omen Machine): each shape is identical at every tag from v0.66.0 to v0.134.0.
  - The 208 parked (unsupported, non-census) pins, scanned automatically: 72 have a residue-free record at some older tag. I sampled 7 of them (Legion of Clay, Alex Wilder, Lightning Runner, Karn, Living Legacy, Heartseeker, Assassin's Ink, Fumble). Six older typings were wrong:
    - a content-free `Pump` on `Any`;
    - a dropped condition;
    - a swallowed-clause warning;
    - a hollow emblem;
    - a dropped artifact clause.
  - Heartseeker's v0.94.0 grant looks right. It is now demoted to `granter_reference_unreached`, the deliberate, tested fail-closed rule the 2026-11-25 vetting found for Necromancy (`self_grant_refused_name_demotes_its_grant`).
  - That matches the 2026-11-25 finding that phase moved wrong typings to fail closed. The other 65 were NOT read one by one, so "parked, not a regression" rests on that sample.
