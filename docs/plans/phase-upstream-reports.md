# phase-rs parse problems to report upstream

Drafted 2026-10-31 against **phase-rs v0.118.0** (`mtg_utils._phase.PHASE_TAG`). Dan posts these to
phase-rs/phase; nothing here has been filed. Each numbered section is one parse *shape*, written to be
pasted as an issue: the cards (name, set), the observed parse (a fragment of phase's own face record, as
`phase_records_for` returns it), the expected parse in phase's own vocabulary, and a one-line suggestion
where the grammar lacks a field. Under each report sit the other cards the backlog names for the same
shape. Section 21 is a census of phase's `Unimplemented` residue classes, for reporting by class rather than per card;
Appendix A lists every ledger row (`bridge_ledger.py`) that records a phase bug, grouped by shape; the
"Already fixed" section lists items the backlog still carries that phase has since fixed; the last table
maps each backlog entry to the sections here.

**How each item was checked.** (1) The bridge ledger's 931 pin cases (`tests/mtg-utils/test_bridge_ledger.py`) and all 166
`retirement_canary` tests pass on this tree, so every ledger pin and every canary-guarded card still shows its
problem at v0.118.0. (2) For the other cards a script read the raw phase face record and the fragment is
quoted. Fragments are abridged (null/false fields dropped, long fields cut with `…`). "(ledger)" or "(canary)"
after a card means it is covered by (1) and its fragment is not re-quoted; "(backlog, not re-checked)" means
the backlog names it but this pass did not look at its record. Set codes are the printing the card pool
resolved (cheapest priced printing), not necessarily the first.

Cards in these sections are legal in at least one format; no playtest-only or Un-set cards.

## 1. Graveyard arrival "from anywhere other than the battlefield" parsed as an `Unknown` trigger mode

**Report.** Disa the Restless (DSK): the first triggered ability parses with `mode: {Unknown: "Whenever
a Lhurgoyf permanent card is put into your graveyard from anywhere other than the battlefield"}`, `origin`/`destination`/`valid_card` null, while the body is
typed (`ChangeZone` of `ParentTarget` to `Battlefield`). Expected: `mode:
ChangesZone`, `destination: Graveyard`, `valid_card: Typed[Permanent,
{Subtype: Lhurgoyf}] + Owned You`. Note the grammar has no origin for 'from anywhere other than the battlefield' (origin null today means 'from anywhere',
as on Countryside Crusher); an `origin: NotBattlefield` or an excluded-origin
field would let the clause be typed exactly.

*(The paragraph above is the report text already written in the backlog's tutor-read entry, verbatim. Re-checked at v0.118.0: Disa's trigger is `{"mode": {"Unknown": "Whenever a Lhurgoyf permanent card is put into your graveyard from anywhere other than the battlefield"}, "execute": {"effect": {"type": "ChangeZone", "destination": "Battlefield", "target": {"type": "ParentTarget"}}}}`; Countryside Crusher's "from anywhere" watcher is `{"mode": "ChangesZone", "valid_card": {"type": "Typed", "type_filters": ["Land"], "properties": [{"type": "Owned", "controller": "You"}]}, "destination": "Graveyard"}`.)*

Affected: Disa the Restless (M3C/DSK). Workaround: `_card_ir/misparsed_shapes.py` graveyard-arrival row, canary `test_graveyard_arrival_unknown_mode_canary`.

## 2. Trigger events phase can't type, parked as an `Unknown` mode

**Report.** Several triggers parse with `mode: {Unknown: "<the trigger text>"}` and no event fields, while their effect is typed. Eye of Singularity (VIS): `"mode": {"Unknown": "Whenever a permanent other than a basic land enters"}, "execute": {"effect": {"type": "DestroyAll", "target": {"type": "Typed", "type_filters": ["Permanent"], "properties": [{"type": "Another"}]}, "cant_regenerate": true}}` (the same-name narrowing is also gone, see §19). Ranar the Ever-Watchful (KHC): `"mode": {"Unknown": "Whenever one or more cards are put into exile from your hand or a spell or ability you control exiles one or more permanents from the battlefield"}` with a typed Spirit `Token` body. Expected: a typed event (`ChangesZone` Hand→Exile for the first half of Ranar's compound event, an enters watcher with an excluded-basic-land filter for Eye). Suggestion: a compound "A or B" trigger event could be a list of modes on one trigger, and `ChangesZone` already has the vocabulary for Ranar's first half. The census (§21) finds 172 commander-legal cards with an `Unknown` trigger mode.

Also (ledger, CI-verified): Hero of Bretagard (exile-from-hand compound, as Ranar), Nicanzil, Current Conductor ("explores a land card / a nonland card" should be an `Explored` watcher), Dream Devourer ("Whenever you foretell a card"), The Fourth Doctor ("When you do, create a Food token": `Unknown` mode "when you do" with no `execute`), Sacred Ground ("a spell or ability an opponent controls causes a land to be put into your graveyard from the battlefield"), Thrasher Brute ("another Warrior your team controls enters"), Sower of Discord ("Whenever damage is dealt to one of the chosen players"; its "choose two players" also parks), and the Attraction cards (backlog: "Attraction Unknown modes", not individually named).

## 3. Tracked set lost or unbound ("destroyed this way", "cast it from your graveyard")

**Report.** Cleansing Meditation (TOR): "Threshold — … instead destroy all enchantments, then return all cards in your graveyard destroyed this way to the battlefield." parses the return as `{"type": "ChangeZoneAll", "origin": "Graveyard", "destination": "Battlefield", "target": {"type": "Typed", "type_filters": ["Card"], "controller": "You", "properties": [{"type": "InZone", "zone": "Graveyard"}]}}`, i.e. every card in your graveyard. The set the preceding `DestroyAll` produced ("destroyed this way") is not carried. Expected: the `ChangeZoneAll` targets the tracked set of the preceding destroy (`TrackedSet`, as `GrantCastingPermission` uses). Skyclave Shade (PZNR): "you may cast it from your graveyard this turn" parses as `GrantCastingPermission {permission: {type: PlayFromExile, mode: Cast}, target: {type: TrackedSet}}` on a tracked set nothing filled, with a `SourceInZone: Graveyard` condition, when the card itself (`SelfRef`, from the graveyard) is the object. Expected: a graveyard-cast permission on `SelfRef`.

Also: Global Ruin ("chooses from the lands they control a land of each basic land type, then sacrifices the rest" parses as `Sacrifice{TrackedSet, 1}`; Cataclysm gets `ChooseAndSacrificeRest`; ledger), Hildibrand Manderville, Mosswood Dreadknight, Ichor Aberration (same "cast it from your graveyard" shape as Skyclave Shade; canary `test_unbound_graveyard_cast_misparse_canary`), King Narfi's Betrayal ("exile a creature or planeswalker card from each graveyard" is a `ChooseFromZone` into a `TrackedSet`; backlog, not re-checked).

## 4. "Unless [player] pays …" lost or unsupported

**Report.** Rhystic Tutor (PCY): "Unless any player pays {2}, search your library for a card, put that card into your hand, then shuffle." parses as an ability whose head effect is `{"type": "Unimplemented", "name": "empty", "description": null}` carrying `"unless_pay": {"cost": {"type": "Mana", "cost": {"type": "Cost", "shards": [], "generic": 2}}, "payer": {"type": "AllPlayers"}}`, with `SearchLibrary` → `ChangeZone` Library→Hand → `Shuffle` hung beneath it as `sub_ability` nodes. The payment is typed (cost and payer), but it sits on a placeholder effect and the search itself carries no `unless_pay`. Question and suggestion: is a head `Unimplemented "empty"` with `unless_pay` meant to gate its `sub_ability` chain at runtime? If so the record is working as designed and only the placeholder name is misleading; if not, the `unless_pay` belongs on the `SearchLibrary` (as it is on `Counter` for "unless its controller pays"). Geistlight Snare (VOW) shows the typed form: `Counter {target: StackSpell}` with `unless_pay: {cost: Mana {3}, payer: ParentTargetController}`. Rune Snag (UMA): "Counter target spell unless its controller pays {2} plus an additional {2} for each card named Rune Snag in each graveyard" is parked whole as `Unimplemented {name: "unless_payment"}` (the dynamic cost is not representable). Withercrown (ledger): a granted "you lose N life unless you sacrifice ~" body is parked as `Unimplemented("Unsupported unless clause")`; the census (§21) finds 34 commander-legal cards with that residue name.

Also: Nether Void's unless-pay (backlog, not re-checked), Distant Memories (MBS): "Any opponent may have you put that card into your hand. If no player does, you draw three cards." is typed at v0.118.0 (`optional: true, optional_for: AnyOpponent` on the put-into-hand, draw conditioned on `Not OptionalEffectPerformed`); not a defect, see "Already fixed".

## 5. A reveal-only search parsed as a move to hand; the same-name rider parked

**Report.** Grim Reminder (MRD): "Search your library for a nonland card and reveal it. Each opponent who cast a spell this turn with the same name as that card loses 6 life. Then shuffle." parses as `SearchLibrary {reveal: true}` → `ChangeZone {origin: Library, destination: Hand, target: Any}` → `Unimplemented {name: "unbound_subject", description: "who cast a spell this turn with the same name as that card loses 6 life"}` (with `player_scope: Opponent`) → `Shuffle`. The card is revealed, not put into hand, so the `ChangeZone` to `Hand` is spurious, and the loss-of-life clause is parked. Expected: `SearchLibrary` with `reveal: true` and no destination move; the clause as `LoseLife 6` for each opponent matching a `CastSpellThisTurn` same-name filter. Chaos Mutation and Chaotic Transformation (ledger): a `RevealUntil` whose revealer and putter differ ("its controller reveals … puts that card onto the battlefield") types `kept_destination` as `Hand` instead of `Battlefield`.

## 8. `where X is …` binding residue that swallows the whole effect

Also: The Fall of Cair Andros (amass X; backlog, card not in the pool at this checkout), Concert Kaboomist, Dwarven Shrine, Power Surge, Psychic Allergy (ledger: "deals X damage to that player, where X is …" keeps only the residue), Huntmaster Liger (ledger: mutate-count team pump), Desmond Miles (where-X scry/surveil; backlog, not re-checked), Custodi Soulcaller and Venerable Warsinger (§7).
